How a workspace gets its name

Works the same for English and 中文 workflows

01 · New workspace

Wait for a real title

Orca names a new workspace from your first message to its agent. Naming Guard waits for that instead of guessing.

  • Random slugs are left as they are
  • Optional standby name, e.g. Mac｜待命工作区 1a2b3c4d
  • Older workspaces only get suggestions

02 · Title arrives

Add the project prefix

As soon as Orca titles the workspace, the project name goes in front, in one consistent style.

  • Prefix from the project, or from your alias
  • Generic prefixes such as Agent｜ are fixed
  • Meaningful branches name worktrees directly

03 · Your call

Your name always wins

Rename a workspace yourself and Naming Guard never touches it again. Every change can be undone.

  • A manual rename locks the workspace
  • Undo and Ignore lock it too
  • Every rename is listed in the panel
